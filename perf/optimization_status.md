This is the running record of every measurement pass and experiment. Raw output belongs under `perf/results/`;
conclusions belong here. The notebook is append-only and ordered oldest first: never rewrite or reorder an entry.

The verdict is one of:

- **LANDED:** kept, in the code.
- **KEPT:** kept behind a knob.
- **REJECTED**
- **CANDIDATE:** awaiting the full check.
- **DEFERRED**
- **INCONCLUSIVE**
- **MIXED**
- **RECORDED:** a measurement, not a change.

After appending, run `python3 perf/harness/perf_diff.py index perf/optimization_status.md`.

Status: landed tooling.

This entry adds `perf/` on the model of the QuixiCore CUDA handbook (`~/git/QuixiCore/QuixiCore-CUDA/perf/`),
adapted from kernel benchmarks to an end-to-end engine. It contains:

- the handbook `perf.md`;

- `backlog.md`, a beam of five families;
- `scoreboard.md`;
- `harness/bench.sh`, which runs the standard cases and handles the memory cap, the GPU-free waits, the build

The keep bars (3% low-risk, 8% complexity-adding) and the noise rule (no decision inside the repeats' spread) are

11.7-12.4 ms per token.

Open questions: see `perf.md` "Open Items", in particular the git-ignored token files and the missing long-context
and serving cases.

Open questions: what drives decode's spread? Candidates are NVMe timing (the fetch counter) and promotion timing.
